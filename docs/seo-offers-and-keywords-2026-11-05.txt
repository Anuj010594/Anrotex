Keyword analysis
Evidence: Search Console Web, main www site, all countries/devices. Latest
complete 28 days: 6 October–2 November; latest 7 days: 27 October–2 November.
All figures below are this site's reported impressions, not total search
volume. Visible query rows omit some searches. No keyword-volume provider,
paid keyword tool, current competitor SERP study or country-specific volume
estimate was used. These priorities are evidence-based opportunities, not
forecasts or promises of increased traffic.

Priority 1 — reduce aws bill / reduce aws costs
28 days: 136 impressions at position 19.5 / 123 at 22.3; zero reported clicks.
Latest week: 27 at 13.2 / 29 at 17.3, versus 24 at 20.3 / 18 at 19.9.
Why: the largest observed search exposure and clear relevance to the cost
review. These phrases show a problem to solve, though they do not establish
an intention to hire a consultancy.
Destination: /blog/reduce-aws-costs, then the existing AWS review.
Implementation: the guide already uses both costs/bill naturally, explains
how to investigate a bill increase and includes EC2/storage/network checks.
The new early offer makes paid help visible after the useful free resource.
Do not create separate pages for reduce aws cost, reduce your aws bill and
aws cost reduction: they largely describe the same need.
Next possible search-snippet experiment, after adequate post-crawl data:
  Reduce Your AWS Bill: Checklist & Cost Worksheet | Anrotex
This is a candidate only. No title change has been implemented in this batch.
Google chooses the displayed title/snippet and can rewrite either.

Application rules
Use one main purpose per page. Article pages answer the technical question;
service pages explain the work Anrotex can deliver. Do not repeat variants
to increase keyword density, add meta-keywords tags, invent city offices or
create separate pages for each of the nine markets. The current all-country
data does not establish demand in any individual target market. Review
country/device breakdowns as more relevant traffic accumulates.
