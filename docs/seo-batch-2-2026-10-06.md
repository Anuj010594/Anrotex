## Implemented changes

- Refreshed the AWS guide title, description, visible update date, and article
  modification date. Kept its URL and original publication date.
- Added a billing review sequence, consistent cost-basis guidance, retained
  costs for stopped resources, and a benefit/risk/verification table.
- Added an explicitly illustrative EC2 rightsizing example. The arithmetic is
  three replicas × 730 hours × hypothetical hourly rates: $438.00 versus $262.80,
  a potential $175.20 compute-only reduction. Assumptions, exclusions, performance
  tests, and rollback requirements are stated. These are not client results or
  quoted AWS prices.
- Added network-cost guidance, corrected Kubernetes requests/limits wording,
  explained commitment overlap, and documented how to measure post-change costs.
  Relevant AWS and Kubernetes primary documentation is linked beside the claims.
- Expanded the audit page with a sample finding, evidence requirements, minimum
  access, scope exclusions, delivery options, and estimated-versus-measured
  savings. Its six FAQ answers are now present in the initial HTML using native
  `details`/`summary`; removed the separate FAQ schema and accordion state.
- Added tracked contextual links between the guide, audit, and fintech case
  study. Updated the blog entry and made article titles tracked links.
- Updated sitemap dates for the four changed public pages only; retained
  canonical URLs. Extended the existing SEO check to validate internal fragments,
  AWS FAQ rendering, and the AWS-focused enquiry selection.

The existing case-study claims of 42% lower spend and 3× release improvement
remain unchanged. The measurement period and underlying before/after records
have not been supplied or independently verified; no additional client metrics
were invented. A request for supporting details remains open.
