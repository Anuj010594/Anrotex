Website policies and download guidance — 3 November 2026

Prepared on main. On 3 November the owner approved this policy batch's one-push
administrator exception, immediate restoration of protection and, specifically,
learning/evaluation-only use. The unpublished commit was amended to honor that
specific choice before the approved push. The earlier exception for correction
41a9200 was not reused.

Public pages
- /privacy-policy: enquiry data, hosting, Turnstile, Google Analytics consent,
  Vercel analytics/performance reports, retention criteria and privacy requests.
- /terms-of-use: examples, sample-code permission, other content, separate paid
  engagements, responsible use and limits subject to non-waivable legal rights.
- /downloads-policy: isolated testing, permissions, cloud charges, differences
  between simulated tests and AWS scripts, fingerprints and concern reporting.

All three pages are linked from the footer, prerendered and in the sitemap.
The enquiry form and cookie panel link to the privacy policy. Download sections
link to safe-use guidance. The Lambda ZIP contains README, TERMS and the three
unchanged Python files. SHA-256 fingerprints are regenerated during each build
and checked against built files and the rendered download policy.

Permission selected by the owner
Anrotex sample code and workflows are limited to learning and evaluation in a
separate test environment, with notices retained. Production use, delivery as
part of client work and redistribution require written permission. A different
licence supplied with a file still governs that file. The wording does not grant
rights to third-party code, branding or whole-article republication.

Limits and drafting choices
These are practical policy drafts, not legal advice or a legal compliance audit.
Have qualified counsel review liability wording, licensing and privacy obligations
for Anrotex's business and target markets. No blanket safety certification or
claim of worldwide privacy compliance is made. A checksum confirms matching bytes,
not safety or authorship. No new live AWS release or dependency security audit was
performed. We did not invent a fixed retention period or a named privacy officer.
Vercel analytics is disclosed as able to run when Google Analytics is declined,
matching the current code; no tracking or consent behavior was changed.
